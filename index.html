About

I've sat in your chair.
That's the difference.

I'm not a consultant who discovered marketing last year, or a marketer who discovered AI last month. I've spent 15+ years inside B2B and fintech companies — as CMO at multiple forex brokers in Cyprus.

That means I know what it's like to own a number, defend a budget, and answer to a board. When I recommend an AI workflow, it's because I've run it inside a real marketing team, under real compliance constraints — not because it demos well.

My view on AI is simple: it should support human decisions, never replace them. The teams winning right now aren't the ones with the most tools. They're the ones whose leaders understand where AI creates leverage and where human judgment is the moat.
